W1700K SDR

Quick start

Your unit arrives with the SDR system installed and no login key of anyone's. Cable it, set up a Linux host, give it your key with a paperclip, and start a stream. No serial console is needed.

  1. Cable it and power it on

    wan to the host's stream port, lan3 to its control port. Wait for the LED to turn white and brighten: red, a rainbow, then yellow while the stream port links (6 s, or 18 s on a USB adapter).

  2. Get the software 
    git clone https://github.com/jstockdale/w1700k-sdr
cd w1700k-sdr && make firmware host
  3. Give the host its two addresses 
    sudo init/host/link keep CONTROL 192.168.1.10/24
sudo init/host/link keep STREAM 10.77.0.10/24 9216

    CONTROL and STREAM are your interface names (ip link). The stream needs the jumbo frames.

  4. Give the unit your key 
    init/key --known-hosts ~/.ssh/known_hosts

    Then hold the reset button with a paperclip. The LED rises white for 10 s, then flashes and pulses blue: let go. It breathes blue, then turns green when your key is in.

    Check the host key fingerprint the command prints against the one above.

  5. Start the receiver 
    host/stream/build/w1700k-stream &
host/spectrum/build/w1700k-spectrum --controller "host/bin/w1700k serve"

    Open http://127.0.0.1:8073/ and press ▶.

The LED

Green go · yellow look · red stop
What you seeWhat it means
Yellow, breathingNo link on the stream port.
White, brighteningPowered and linked; radio not loaded yet.
Green, steadyReady: radio loaded, not streaming.
Cyan, blue or magentaStreaming at 40, 80 or 160 Msps.
The rainbow, round in 6 sStreaming at 320 Msps.
… steadyLossless: every sample bit for bit.
… breathingFewer bits per sample, to fit the link.
… blinking slowlyStarted, but nothing is leaving the port.
Yellow, blinkingOut of lock: tune back into the band.
Red, blinkingThe stream ended by itself.
A short lift towards whiteThe unit heard a change from the host.
Red, never changingDid not finish booting. Cycle the power.

The reset button

While you holdLetting go
Green, risingAt 5 s: switches your key on.
Red, sinkingAt 5 s: switches your key off.
White, rising for 10 sNo key yet: nothing to switch.
5 s: that colour, pulsingSwitches it. Off, the key is kept.
10 s: blue, pulsingTakes a new key: init/key.
15 s: red, blinking hardErases the key.

What your link carries

Stream linkLossless, all 14 bitsReduced to fit
10 Gbit/s320 Msps
5 Gbit/s160 Msps320 Msps at 14 bits per I/Q pair
2.5 Gbit/s80 Msps160 Msps at 14 bits per I/Q pair
1 Gbit/s80 Msps at 8 bits, 40 Msps at 16 bits

The software packs the samples to fit the link. The 2.5 and 1 Gbit/s rows are computed, not yet run.

Read before use. The unit receives only; still, put an attenuator between an antenna port and a sensitive instrument. Only the reset button changes the login key.
